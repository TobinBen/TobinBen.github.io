02 / ABOUT ME

Curious by
profession.

BT↗

I’m a journalist who believes the best business stories are also stories about people and places.

At the Triangle Business Journal, I cover economic development, commercial real estate and local government across North Carolina's fast-changing Greater Triangle region. My work follows investments, incentives, development projects and the communities they affect.

Previously, I covered major retailers for Business Insider and breaking news and business for the Louisville Courier Journal. At the Courier Journal, I was part of the team awarded the 2020 Pulitzer Prize for Breaking News Reporting for coverage of former Kentucky Gov. Matt Bevin's controversial pardons.

I graduated from the University of Virginia, where I studied politics and media studies, with a minor in Spanish.
